FAQ

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is $5.99 and free on orders over $75 after discounts. Express is $14.99, next-day $24.99, and store pickup is free.

Which promo codes work?

WELCOME10 (10% off), ORBIT20 (20% off orders $100+), GAMER15 (15% off gaming items) and FREESHIP (free standard shipping). One code per order.

What is the return policy?

Returns are accepted within 30 days in original condition (demo policy).

Is my cart saved?

Yes — your demo cart is saved in this browser only, so it survives refreshes and closed tabs. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is this a real store?

No. Orbit Lane is a fictional demonstration built by Virellion Apex. No orders are placed and no payments are taken.
